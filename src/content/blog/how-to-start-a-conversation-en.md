Starting a conversation with someone you don't know can be harder than keeping it going. The first message carries a lot of weight — but it doesn't need to be perfect.

## Start with something specific

A plain "hi" is fine, but a conversation tends to move faster when the first message gives the other person something to respond to — a question, an observation, a small detail about yourself.

## You don't need to share a language

One of the biggest barriers to talking with people from other countries is language. In HEYID, messages are translated automatically, so you can write in your own language and the other person reads it in theirs — and vice versa.

## When you don't know what to say

Sometimes you just don't have an opening line, or a reply. That's where SPARX comes in — HEYID's built-in AI assistant, which suggests possible replies when you're stuck. It's help carrying the conversation, not a replacement for the other person.

## Give the conversation time

Not every conversation has to last for hours. Notifications let you know when someone replies, so you can pick things back up later instead of feeling pressure to answer immediately.
