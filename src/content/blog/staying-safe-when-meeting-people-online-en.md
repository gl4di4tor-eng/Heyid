Meeting new people online can be a great experience — but just like in real life, it's worth keeping a few basic safety habits in mind. Here are some things worth remembering.

## Take your time with personal details

You don't need to share your phone number, address, or workplace right away. A good conversation doesn't require that upfront — trust builds gradually, not in the first message.

## Trust your gut

If something in a conversation feels off — pushy questions, pressure, or strange requests — you can simply end the conversation. You don't owe anyone an explanation, and you don't need to stay polite at your own expense.

## Report and block when needed

HEYID lets you block a user at any time and report inappropriate behavior. These are tools worth using without hesitation — no one has to sit through an uncomfortable conversation.

## Meeting in person? Do it smart

If an online conversation turns into wanting to meet in person, choose a public place, let someone close to you know your plans, and take your time if something feels uncertain.

## A language barrier doesn't have to be a trust barrier

Automatic translation in HEYID lets you talk to people from other countries without misunderstandings caused by language — but the same common sense about safety applies no matter where the other person is from.

Talking to someone new is often the best part of meeting people from around the world — it just helps to do it with a bit of awareness.
